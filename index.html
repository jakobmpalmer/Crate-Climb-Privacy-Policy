Crate Climb Privacy Policy

Developer: A to Jump · Effective date: September 14, 2026

This policy explains how Crate Climb handles your information. In short: Crate Climb does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, Crate Climb saves a small amount of information locally on your device:

  • The username you choose to enter
  • Your best score
  • A local high score table of usernames and scores from games played on that device
  • Your settings: vibration, sound effects, music, and the visual style you selected

This information never leaves your device through Crate Climb. The app does not request internet access, so it has no ability to send data to us or to anyone else. We never see or receive it.

Device backup

If backup is enabled in your Android settings, Android may include Crate Climb's saved information in your device's backup, stored in your own Google account. That backup is managed by Android and Google under your account settings, not by us. We have no access to it.

Sharing your score

If you tap the Share button, Crate Climb opens Android's share menu with a short message containing your score, your best score and a link to the game. Nothing is shared unless you choose to do this, and you choose which app receives the message. What happens after that is governed by the privacy policy of the app you share to.

No ads, analytics or tracking

Crate Climb contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and does not include any third-party services that collect data. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or device identifiers.

Children

Crate Climb is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

Because your information is stored only on your device, you control it completely. Uninstalling Crate Climb, or clearing its data in your Android settings, permanently deletes it.

Changes to this policy

If Crate Climb ever begins handling information differently, this policy will be updated before that change reaches you, and the effective date above will change.
